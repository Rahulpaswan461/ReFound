# ReFound privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

ReFound is a Chrome extension that shows forgotten bookmarks on your new tab page.

## What ReFound reads
ReFound reads your Chrome bookmarks (titles, addresses, folders and dates saved) so it can choose what to show you.

## What ReFound stores
ReFound stores a copy of that bookmark information on your device, along with its own history (such as when a bookmark was shown, opened, snoozed or dismissed), your settings, and any background images you upload.

## What ReFound shares
Nothing. ReFound has no server, no account and no analytics. It does not send your bookmarks, history, settings or images to the developer or to anyone else, and it does not sell or transfer your data.

Search text you enter goes to your default search engine through Chrome, and pages you open load from their own websites, as they would in any tab.

## Your control
Settings > Your data > Clear ReFound data removes ReFound's history, settings and uploaded images. Removing the extension removes its stored data. Neither action changes your Chrome bookmarks.

## Limited Use
ReFound's use of information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
