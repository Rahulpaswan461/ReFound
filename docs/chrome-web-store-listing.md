# Chrome Web Store listing text

## Short description (from the manifest)
Rediscover the bookmarks you saved and forgot, every time you open a new tab.

## Category
Productivity (Tools / Workflow)

## Detailed description
You save great things and never go back to them. ReFound quietly brings them back.

Every day, ReFound picks a few bookmarks you've probably forgotten and shows them on your new tab page. Read one now, remind yourself later, or dismiss it. There's nothing new to learn: keep bookmarking the way you always have.

- A few picks a day, never a to-do list
- Once you've acted on a pick, later tabs show the rest on one quiet line, and you can pause any day
- Search the web and your bookmarks from one box
- A searchable library with your folders
- Beautiful backgrounds, or upload your own
- Your bookmarks stay on your device: no account, no server, no tracking

ReFound never changes or deletes your Chrome bookmarks.

## Single purpose
ReFound replaces the new tab page to help people rediscover bookmarks they saved and forgot.

## Permission justifications
- bookmarks: Reads the user's Chrome bookmarks to choose daily picks and show the library. ReFound never writes to them.
- search: Sends searches from the new tab search box to the user's default search engine.
- storage: Saves the user's settings on their device.
- unlimitedStorage: Stores the local bookmark history and uploaded background images on the device.
- favicon: Shows each saved site's icon from Chrome's local cache.
- Remote code: No. All code ships inside the extension.

## Test instructions
No login is needed. Install, choose folders on the setup screen, and open a new tab. Bookmarks at least 7 days old are preferred; newer ones are used if none qualify.
